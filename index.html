Our Approach

How a Top Immigration Lawyer Should Work With You

Many people search for the best immigration lawyer and are unsure what that really means. In our view, a trusted immigration lawyer does a few things consistently. First, the attorney listens before advising. Your history, your family and your goals shape the strategy, not a generic template. Second, the attorney explains the law in simple terms and tells you what is realistic. Third, the attorney prepares every filing as if it will be examined closely, because it will be.

We begin each matter with a detailed consultation. We ask about your entry, your status, your travel, your family ties and any past applications or encounters with immigration authorities. This conversation often reveals options that clients did not know existed, and it also reveals problems that are better handled early. From there, we build a plan, give you a checklist of documents and explain the timeline.

As your case proceeds, we monitor filings, respond to requests for evidence and prepare you for interviews or hearings. If something unexpected happens, such as a notice, a denial or a detention, you will not be left guessing about what comes next. We also believe in teaching clients how the system works, so you can make choices with confidence long after your case closes.

FAQs

Immigration Lawyer Questions Answered

How do I choose the right immigration lawyer in Brooklyn?

Look for an immigration attorney who is licensed, has experience with your type of case, communicates clearly and explains fees and risks up front. Meeting for a consultation is a good way to judge whether the attorney listens and gives honest answers.

What should I bring to my first consultation?

Bring your passport, any immigration documents you hold, notices from USCIS or the immigration court, copies of past applications and a short timeline of your travel and status history. If you do not have everything, come anyway and we will tell you what to gather.

How long does a green card application take?

Processing time depends on the category, your country of birth, the filing location and agency workload. Some cases take several months and others take years. An attorney can explain the current estimates that apply to your situation.

Can you help if a family member has been detained by ICE?

Yes. Call our office as soon as possible. We can review the situation, discuss bond and defense options and represent the person in immigration court. Time matters in detention cases, so quick action is important.

Do you offer immigration services in languages other than English?

Yes. Our team serves clients in English, Spanish and Russian, so you can discuss your case in the language you are most comfortable with.

Can I apply for citizenship if I have a green card?

Many permanent residents become eligible after a required period of residence, commonly five years or three years for some spouses of citizens, if they meet other requirements such as good moral character and physical presence. We review your history before you file.

Is DACA still an option for me?

DACA rules and court rulings have changed several times. Our DACA lawyer can review your current status, explain what filings are available and discuss whether other forms of relief might apply to you.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash, checks and all major credit cards.

Asylum Lawyer

Private, respectful help building a strong asylum claim, including withholding of removal and torture protection.

Read more

Asylum protects people who have suffered persecution or fear it because of race, religion, nationality, political opinion or membership in a particular social group. An asylum case depends heavily on your story, your evidence and how clearly both are presented. Our asylum lawyer team meets with you in a private and respectful setting, listens to your experience and helps you prepare a detailed declaration. We gather country condition reports, witness statements, medical or psychological records and any documents that support your claim. We also pay close attention to filing deadlines, since the one year rule can affect eligibility. In addition to asylum, we evaluate withholding of removal and protection under the Convention Against Torture. We prepare you for the asylum interview or the court hearing and explain what to expect each step of the way. For young people, we also review options such as Special Immigrant Juvenile status. Safety and dignity come first in our work with asylum clients.

Special Immigrant Juvenile (SIJ)

Guidance for young people who may qualify for Special Immigrant Juvenile status and permanent residence.

Read more

Special Immigrant Juvenile status helps certain young people in the United States who have been abused, abandoned or neglected by one or both parents. It can lead to lawful permanent residence, and it requires careful coordination between family court and immigration authorities. Our Brooklyn immigration attorney team explains the process, reviews whether the young person may qualify and works with you on the steps required before filing with the immigration agency. We gather court orders, identity records and supporting evidence, prepare the petition and, when appropriate, the green card application that follows. Timing matters, because eligibility is tied to age and to how the state court order is issued, so we encourage families and guardians to contact us early. We treat these cases with sensitivity and patience, and we explain everything in language that young clients and their caregivers can understand. Our team speaks English, Spanish and Russian, which helps guardians take part in every step. Call us for a confidential consultation.

Work Permits and Travel Documents

Work permit applications, renewals, replacements and travel documents such as advance parole.

Read more

Many people need permission to work while their immigration case is pending or after they receive a particular status. Our team helps clients apply for employment authorization documents, often called work permits, in categories such as pending green card applications, asylum, DACA, temporary protected status and others. We confirm which category applies, prepare the application, gather the required photographs and evidence and file it correctly. We also assist with renewals, since filing too late can interrupt your ability to work. If your card was lost, stolen or contains an error, we help you request a replacement or correction. Beyond work permits, we assist with travel documents such as advance parole, which can matter greatly if you need to leave the country and return. Traveling without the right document can put a pending case at risk, so please speak with an attorney before booking a flight. Our immigration help covers the paperwork, deadlines and follow up, so you can focus on your job and your family.
